About

I’m Jack, a product and service design lead. Over 15 years in tech has taken me from commercial roles at GroupM and Salesforce, through hands-on product design, to leading design teams across EMEA and APAC.

Outside of work I think a lot about attention, connection and our relationship with technology. It’s become the thread running through everything I do: technology that fosters healthier relationships with our devices, the planet and each other.
